Help

  • Dates: The calendar used in this software is based on the Ethiopian Calendar (EC). There are 12 months, each with 30 days. The thirteenth month in the EC, which has 5/6 days, is omitted.
     
  • Printing: When printing a page, it may be better to print it landscape rather than portrait. To change the setting to landscape, click on the 'File' menu, then select 'Page Setup'. In the 'Orientation' section change the selection to 'Landscape'. Alternatively, the page can be sent to Excel and then printed.
     
  • Backup: The database should be regularly backed up by the user. If you need to restore a database, then please contact the ICT Directorate of Bureau of Finance.
     
  • For any questions, please contact the ICT Directorate at BoF or send an email to beladarik@gmail.com or Post on the telegram Group SER_BoF_ICT or Call 0926538705
